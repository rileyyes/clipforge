# Effects: bleeps, comedy edits & captions (Phase 4)

Everything here is free and runs on your PC. Open a project in the **Manual Editor** and click the **Effects** tab, next to *Clip*.

## 1 · Transcribe (needed for bleeps and captions)

One-time install, in the `backend` folder with the `.venv` active:

```
pip install -r requirements-ai.txt
```

Restart ClipForge, then click **Transcribe speech**. The first run downloads the Whisper "small" model (~250 MB). It runs on the CPU by default. On an NVIDIA GPU with CUDA installed, set `CLIPFORGE_WHISPER_DEVICE=cuda` for a big speed-up. `CLIPFORGE_WHISPER_MODEL=base` is faster, `medium` more accurate.

> Testing note: the bleep/caption pipeline is fully tested with word timings shaped like Whisper's output. The faster-whisper call itself couldn't be installed in the build environment, so your first real transcription is its first real run. Tell me if it errors.

## 2 · Bleep swears

Pick a sound (**beep, boing, record scratch, honk, quack** or **mute**), choose *strong swears* or *+ mild* (damn/hell/crap), optionally add your own words, then click **Find & bleep swears**.

- The swear is **removed from the audio** under the sound. It isn't just covered up, so nothing leaks through (tested by frequency analysis).
- Each bleep shows the word, how sure the transcriber was, and the surrounding words. Low-confidence or very short words are marked **check**. Untick a bleep to keep that word, or use ◀+ / +▶ to widen it.
- Captions and the `.srt` file show censored words masked (`S***`).
- All sounds are generated by ClipForge itself (synthesised), so there are no copyright problems.

## 3 · Comedy edits

**Auto-place** (minimal / balanced / chaotic) puts punch-in zooms, screen shakes, pop-up text ("WAIT WHAT?", "BRUH", "NOPE"…) and sound effects on the loudest reactions in your final edit. The pop-up text is picked from nearby words when there's a transcript. Re-running replaces only the auto-placed effects; yours are kept.

**Add at playhead:** Zoom, Shake, Flash, Pop-up text, Sound (boom, pop, whoosh, ding, boing, airhorn…), Bleep, or **Image** (upload your own meme/PNG/GIF once, then reuse it). Images are never added automatically.

Every effect is pinned to a moment in the original footage, so it moves with the clip when you trim or rearrange, and it disappears if you cut that moment. Effects show as coloured diamonds on the timeline ruler, preview in the monitor (zoom, shake, flash, text, images, bleeps), and are fully rendered on export. Undo/redo covers effects too.

## 4 · Captions

Tick **Burn animated captions into the export**, then choose a style: **Bold pop** (pop-in animation), **Word highlight** (karaoke-style), or **Clean box**. You can also set the position, words per line and size. **Download .srt** gives YouTube a separate caption file.

## Fonts

Pop-up text and captions use Impact (Windows/macOS), falling back to Arial Black, Arial Bold or DejaVu Sans Bold. To use another font, set `CLIPFORGE_FONT=C:\path\to\font.ttf`.
